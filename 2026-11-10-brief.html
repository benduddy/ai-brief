The regime

High-yield spreads are 3.48%, up 0.47 points in 50 days, so credit is worsening. The dollar is 2.6% above its 200-day average. The VIX is 25.6. The real 10-year yield is 2.60%. Gold is not diverging from rates. Both the credit and rate legs use real FRED series. Calibration: this gate catches big drawdowns early but fires often, with close to no edge over the base rate. How to read this. Neutral, balanced read — bull and bear side by side. The Setup lines are opinions on risk/reward and timing to weigh for yourself, not directives; the call is yours.

Score key. Heat: 0 = far below its highs and its 50-day (damaged; historically these fell further), 100 = a strong run near its highs (historically no extra pullback risk). Momentum: strength of the recent up-move. Status: Leading = strong and healthy, Overheated = strong and stretched (not a pullback signal), Basing = weak, not yet a bottom, Neutral = no clear signal. ATR: the name’s normal daily swing. Forward PEG: forward P/E divided by expected EPS growth; under ~1 is cheap for the growth, over ~2 expensive. Entry grades A/B/C all pass the trend and relative-strength gates; the letter shows where the price sits against its short-term averages and is not a rank.
