How to read this: Neutral, balanced read — bull and bear side by side. The Setup lines are opinions on risk/reward and timing to weigh for yourself, not directives; the call is yours.

Score key: Heat 0 = far below its highs and its 50-day (damaged; such names historically fell further), 100 = a strong run near its highs (historically no extra pullback risk). Momentum = strength of the recent up-move. Status: Leading = strong and healthy; Overheated = strong and stretched (not a pullback signal); Basing = weak, not yet a bottom; Neutral = no clear signal. ATR = the name’s normal daily swing. Forward PEG = price/earnings divided by expected growth. Entry grades A/B/C pass both momentum gates; the letter shows where price sits against short-term averages and is not a rank. D = failed a gate.
