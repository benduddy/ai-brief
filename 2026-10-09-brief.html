Score key: Heat 0 = far below its highs and its 50-day (damaged; historically these fell further), 100 = a strong run near its highs (historically no extra pullback risk). ATR = the name's normal daily swing. Forward PEG = forward P/E ÷ expected EPS growth; under ~1 cheap for its growth, over ~2 expensive.
