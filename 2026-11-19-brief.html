Earnings and events

Nvidia Q3 FY27, from the company release (17 Nov, 8-K): revenue $115.8B (+103% y/y) vs ~$109.1B (LSEG via CNBC); non-GAAP EPS $2.68; data center $107.6B; gross margin 74.9%. Q4 guide $130B ±3% vs ~$125.1B, no China data-center revenue, and a gross-margin guide of 71–72% (down ~3 points; management ties it to the early Rubin ramp). Supply commitments rose to $390B from $279B, mostly multi-year memory. Buyback authorisation +$150B.

What the balance sheet says: inventory $39.7B (from $21.4B in January) and receivables $81.0B (from $38.5B). Days to collect rose to ~64 from ~60, tied to longer terms on large deals (Panabee summary of the CFO commentary; not checked against the 10-Q). Guarantees up to $118B, $105B of them tied to SB Energy; no payments so far.

Connecting it: demand is not the question. F3 (hyperscaler spend) is intact: Nvidia pre-buys $390B of supply because buyers still order. The market priced three other things: the key-person gap, China, and a balance sheet that now carries its customers (receivables, guarantees). The $390B memory pre-buy is also F2 support (DRAM demand). The day’s move says investors prefer to own the AI demand through AMD, Broadcom and Arista, not through the name that finances it.
