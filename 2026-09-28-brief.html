How to read this. Neutral, balanced read — bull and bear side by side. The Setup lines are opinions on risk/reward and timing to weigh for yourself, not directives; the call is yours.

Score key. Heat — 0 = far below its highs and its 50-day average, i.e. damaged, and historically these fell further; 100 = a strong run near its highs, historically with no extra pullback risk. Momentum — strength of the recent up-move. Status bands — Leading = strong and healthy; Overheated = strong but stretched, which is not a pullback signal; Basing = weak and not yet a bottom; Neutral = no clear signal. ATR — the name's normal daily swing. Forward PEG — price/earnings divided by expected growth; under ~1 means you are paying little for a lot of growth, over ~2 the opposite. Entry grades — A/B/C all pass both momentum gates; the letter is where the price sits against its short-term averages, not a rank. Grades run on the real exported trend fields; they are a timing lens, not a verdict on the business.
