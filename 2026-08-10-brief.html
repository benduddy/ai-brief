Monday Signal Brief

10 August 2026 · 50 names · benchmark QQQ · data Fri close · 07:11 UTC

After a week that punished the high-beta second tier, Friday flipped the script: a broad broadening bounce, with optical-networking leading the charge and the beaten-down names snapping back — while, tellingly, Alphabet went the other way.

  • Friday was a broad second-tier bounce. The names that got hit hardest last week snapped back the most: optical/networking ripped — COHR +13.4% (flipped to Leading), AAOI +9.2%, LITE +6.2%, GLW +5.4% — and rare-earth (USAR +11%, UUUU +9.6%, MP +7.6%) and neoclouds (IREN +8.7%, CRWV +6.3%) all bounced. This is the mirror image of the tier-2 selloff the last brief reported.
  • Alphabet diverged the wrong way. GOOGL flipped Leading→Basing (momentum −22) while everything around it bounced — the one leader that didn't participate. That fits the rotation the followed sources keep flagging: money favouring the supplier/infrastructure layer (chips, optical, memory, power) over the hyperscalers themselves.
  • Value-accumulation bucket (quiet tape, cheap for strong forward growth). In the AI-compute baskets the model flags MU (PEG ~0.05), AMD (0.30), MRVL (0.65), TSM (0.68), ASML (0.83), ALAB (0.96) — all basing, all cheap for big FY-out growth. Low momentum here is a feature (early, not late), not a disqualifier.
  • Portfolio-vs-index gap worth noting. The S&P closed Friday at a fresh record (~7,758, within ~1% of its high), yet the 48-name AI universe sits ~38% below its own 60-day highs on average. Index-level calm doesn't transfer to this portfolio.
  • Score key — heat: 0 = washed-out, 100 = red-hot/overextended. Momentum: strength of the recent up-move. Status: Leading = strong & healthy, Overheated = strong but stretched, Basing = weak/bottoming, Neutral = no clear signal. COHR · Coherent$379.13
LeadingEntry C · parabolicOptical / NetworkingEarnings Tue

Bullish

The day's standout — up 13.4% and flipped from Basing to Leading. The optical/CPO supply chain is where Friday's leadership sat, and Coherent is the diversified giant of it: an Nvidia $2B strategic tie-up plus a multibillion CPO supply agreement, data-centre & comms now ~75% of revenue, book-to-bill above 4x, and InP laser capacity set to double by year-end. Q4 earnings land this Tuesday 12 Aug after the close.

Bearish

A parabolic 13% pop straight into an earnings print is the definition of a hot entry — the setup can be right and the timing still wrong if the report disappoints or the guide merely meets a now-elevated bar. RSI 64 and ~10% above its 50-day baseline leave little cushion if the market fades the move.

Valuation

Forward P/E ~45, next-year EPS growth ~54%, forward PEG ~0.84 — its price/earnings divided by expected growth; under ~1 means you're paying little for a lot of growth, over ~2 the opposite. So even after the run, it screens as fairly-to-cheaply priced for its growth — the move isn't purely multiple expansion.

Forward view

Management is guiding new engines — optical circuit switching, co-packaged and near-packaged optics — toward a $20B+ incremental addressable market by 2030, with InP capacity doubling again into 2027. If CPO ramps as the hyperscalers wire up next-gen clusters, today's price is discounting a fraction of that; the bottleneck story (optics as the pinch-point of scale-up) is a structural tailwind, not a one-quarter trade.

Levels

Trading ~$379, roughly 10% above its 50-day average (~$343) and ~11% below its 52-week high (~$427). The 50-day is the line that matters — a pullback that holds it keeps the new Leading trend intact; a close back below it would say Friday's flip was a head-fake.

Setup

Model: heat 51, grade C (parabolic) — best-in-class trend but the entry is hot right into a binary event. Risk/reward favours waiting for the print rather than chasing the +13% candle: a post-earnings pullback toward the ~$343 50-day would be the cleaner add if the trend holds; a strong report that gaps and holds is the confirmation-buyer's entry. Not a chase here.
