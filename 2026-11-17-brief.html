Macro regime

The model reads deflationary-stress again (it read the same on 6 Nov, then went back to neutral). The drivers: high-yield bond spreads 3.23%, up 0.14 points over 50 days (FRED; rising spreads mean lenders want more pay for risk); the dollar 3.5% above its 200-day average; VIX 22.9. Gold is 13% under its 200-day and there is no gold-versus-rates split. In the rotation framework this is the “deflationary bust” column: credit widens, the dollar rises, gold can fall at first with everything else. It is also the scenario where a rotation into BTC fails. Calibration: this gate caught past big falls early but fires often with close to no edge. Read it as “conditions rhyme with past stress”, not as risk-off. Lagging context: NFCI −0.46 (as of 6 Nov; negative = looser than average).
