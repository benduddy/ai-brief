Earnings and events

Nvidia (CNBC, 12 Nov): Huang out with immediate effect; Kress interim CEO; Jones chair. Bernstein cut its target from $400 to $200; one adviser called the stock “uninvestable” with no succession plan. The stock recovered most of a 14% fall by the close.

Nebius Q3 (12 Nov, before the open; press-sourced, not checked against the 6-K): revenue $980.8M vs ~$907M expected, but ~$325M was one-off. 2026 revenue range cut to $3.0–3.2B because third-party colocation slipped into 2027. 2026 capex up to ~$27B; 2027 capex $40–45B; 2027 revenue guide $12–13B. Contracted power above 4 GW. Funding: $7.4B cash, $5.75B converts in August, ~$1.0B sold through the share program. Outlets tied the drop to the lower 2026 range, the larger loss and the capex. EPS figures conflict between sources, so I do not quote them.

Applied Materials FQ4 (12 Nov, after the close; press-sourced): revenue $10.41B (+53% y/y) vs ~$10.30B; EPS $4.16 vs ~$4.06. Guide $11.1B / $4.35 for a 14-week quarter. DRAM and leading-edge logic fabs are full; advanced packaging up ~90% in 2026 on HBM. China under 25% of sales. After-hours move was about flat.

Connecting them: both prints say demand is not the limit. Space, power and fab capacity are. Nebius lost revenue to late colocation, not to weak orders; AMAT says clean rooms gate the industry. That supports F1/F2/F3 staying intact. The new risk is the financing channel: Nebius needs $40–45B of 2027 capex and plans to fund 75–85% from prepayments and asset-backed debt. That is the channel F4 watches.
