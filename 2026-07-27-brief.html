TAPE · AI-trade & macro backdrop
Week of 27–31 Jul

Where we start

Stocks reopen today after a rough week — the Nasdaq 100 fell about 2.9%, dragged by Friday's Alphabet and Tesla reports, where investors balked at surging AI infrastructure costs. Futures point to a mixed-to-defensive open, with oil back in the high-$70s and Treasury yields firm keeping buyers cautious. This isn't a market falling apart; it's one digesting the bill for the AI build-out during a low-volume summer.

The capex gauntlet

The week is unusually loaded. Wednesday 29 Jul: the Fed decides at 2:00pm ET (a hold is the base case; see below), then Microsoft and Meta both report after the close — the market will read Microsoft's Azure growth and its data-center capex plan especially closely. Thursday 30 Jul: Amazon and Apple follow. After Google's capex-shock print on Friday, these four are the direct test of whether the AI-spending worry was a Google-specific story or an industry-wide one.

Rates & oil

A "hawkish hold" at 3.50–3.75% is widely expected Wednesday, but sticky inflation has pushed the odds of an actual 0.25% hike up to roughly 31–34% — from about 13% a week ago. Oil in the high-$70s on renewed US–Iran tension is the other watch-item; the next CPI print (12 Aug) is where any oil pass-through would first show.
