The one live catalyst in this brief: Q2 lands after tonight's close. AI networking is the engine — the FY26 guide already bakes in $3.5B of AI-driven revenue, and Q1 grew sales ~35% YoY. The new 7060XE7 switch (1.6 terabits/sec, built for AI rack-scale) drove a ~7% rally on launch, and 21 of 26 analysts rate it a strong buy. A clean beat-and-raise would confirm the AI-buildout demand story is still accelerating.

Bearish

Expectations are high and the stock is priced for a good print — a merely in-line quarter can sell off. The specific worry the Street keeps flagging: supply bottlenecks could push out AI-related upside and squeeze gross margins. Signal-wise it's hot going in — heat 56 (heat = how stretched a name looks; 0 = washed-out, 100 = red-hot) and trading ~8% above its 50-day average, near its highs, so there's little cushion if the print disappoints.

Valuation

Forward P/E ~41 (rich), next-year earnings growth ~+23%, forward PEG ~1.81 — that's price-to-earnings divided by expected growth; under ~1 means you're paying little for a lot of growth, over ~2 the opposite. At 1.8 it sits in the "fairly-to-fully priced for its growth" band — the move here is in the trend, not the valuation.

Forward view / TAM

The bull case is that AI back-end networking (the switching fabric between GPUs) is a multi-year, expanding market as clusters scale, and Arista is the merchant-silicon leader taking share from Cisco in the data center. Tonight's number and — more importantly — the forward guide will tell you whether the market has already priced that in (P/E ~41 says a lot is priced) or whether the AI-revenue ramp is running ahead of estimates.

Levels

~$180 now, roughly 8% above its 50-day average (~$167) and only ~3.5% below its 52-week high (~$187). $167 is the line that's kept the uptrend intact; the earnings reaction will likely set the next move well outside these levels either way.

Setup
