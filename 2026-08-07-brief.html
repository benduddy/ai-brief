The tape & the week ahead

The tape

Indices sit near record highs, but under the surface today's session was a rotation out of the high-beta second tier. The mega-cap AI anchors barely moved (Nvidia ~flat, Alphabet and Amazon steady); the damage was concentrated in neoclouds (Nebius −13%, CoreWeave −5%), bitcoin miners (Marathon, CleanSpark ~−5%) and memory (SanDisk −7%). 37 of 50 names moved ≥1% — real two-sided dispersion, a continuation of "leadership narrowing to quality," not a broad crash.

Week ahead

Jobs data landed today (7 Aug); the week's main event is US CPI on 12 Aug, then PPI (13 Aug) and retail sales (14 Aug). Further out, Jackson Hole (27–29 Aug) and the 16–17 Sep FOMC set the rate-path tone. Semis tone is cautious on valuation and the China export overhang — but this month's AI-capex earnings (Arista, Eaton, Dell's backlog) keep confirming the buildout hasn't peaked.

What the smart money is saying (from the wiki)

InvestAnswers (5 Aug, "Whiplash Recovery"): the late-July selloff was overblown, the bottom held, S&P at a fresh high, "PE ~20, no bubble" — but summer stays choppy into Aug/Sept. Today's tier-2 selloff is exactly that promised chop. Gavin Baker (4 Aug, via wiki): "the selloff doesn't match the data" — GPU pricing, DRAM and token growth all accelerating, Nvidia at its lowest forward P/E in a decade, hyperscalers structurally under-earning; his one real worry is regulation (the NY data-center moratorium). Jordi Visser: the mid-cycle slowdown is ending, long-term compute scarcity intact — a "cleansing," not a top. Where they diverge: all three lean bullish-through-the-chop, so respect today's fresh neocloud/memory breakdown as the "rough patch" they warned about — in the high-beta names, not the leaders.
