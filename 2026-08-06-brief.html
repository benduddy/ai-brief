AI-Trade · Daily Brief

Thursday Signal Brief

6 August 2026 · 50 names · benchmark QQQ · data 07:05 UTC · 6 Aug

After yesterday's record melt-up, the AI trade split in two: the leaders held their ground while the second tier took its medicine — and the medicine was mostly earnings.

  • A dispersion day, not a crash. Today's snapshot reflects the 5 Aug session — a quiet-looking index (S&P −0.2%, Nasdaq −0.8%) hiding a violent split underneath. Nvidia (+3.4%) and Arista (+3.6%) rose to or near their highs while AMD (−7%), Astera Labs (−12%), Chemours (−18.6%) and Alphabet (−4%) fell hard — most on their own results. This reads as digestion of a strong run, not a top.
  • Earnings did the sorting. AMD and Astera both beat but sold off on soft guidance / stretched valuation (a classic "sell the news"); Chemours missed and cut its outlook; Arista beat and raised, and broke out. The tape is rewarding clean beat-and-raises and punishing anything priced for perfection.
  • The two "bullseye" names still stand — just don't re-chase them. Nvidia (forward PEG 0.39) and Broadcom (PEG 0.32) remain the rare "leading and still cheap for their growth" combo flagged yesterday, and NVDA was the only mega-cap green today. Nothing new to re-argue here — see yesterday's brief; the point today is they held while others broke.
  • Quiet-tape value is getting cheaper. Micron (forward PEG 0.05, +112% expected growth) sits at ~$893 — right inside Jordi Visser's stated "adding small below $900" zone — with its HBM memory sold out for 2026. AMD (PEG 0.37, +86%) and Marvell (0.62, +54%) join the accumulate-into-weakness bucket — but AMD is a fresh post-earnings knife with no base yet, so it's the riskier of the three.
  • Alphabet is the odd one out — a grade-A trend the value screen can't score. It's the single name sitting at a clean pullback entry (just 1% above its 50-day average), yet its PEG is blank because consensus expects next-year earnings to fall (a capex surge that turned cash flow negative). Strong trend, unscoreable valuation, antitrust overhang — a judgment call, not a screen hit.
  • How to read the labels: heat runs 0 (washed-out / cheap-looking) to 100 (red-hot / overextended); momentum is the strength of the recent up-move; the status bands are Leading (strong & healthy), Overheated (strong but stretched), Basing (weak / bottoming), Neutral (no clear signal). The Setup lines are opinions on risk/reward and timing to weigh for yourself — not directives. The call is yours.

Bullish

The one name that rose and made new highs into the pullback. Bearish

It is now the most stretched name in the book: heat 79, and trading right at its 52-week high with essentially no cushion below. Any wobble in hyperscaler spending, or a rotation out of the winners, hits the most-extended names first. It is priced for continued flawless execution.

Valuation

Not cheap, not egregious — you're paying up for quality and visibility, not getting a bargain.

Forward view

The 800-gigabit networking cycle and rising AI back-end build-out point to a bigger revenue base in 2–3 years (Street models push toward ~$15–16B by 2027). The forward story is strong — but at today's price a lot of that is already in the stock, so this is a "great business, full price" situation rather than an underpriced future.

Levels

Trading ~17% above its 50-day average (~$169) and right on its highs. There's no obvious support until that 50-day; a pullback toward it would be a normal, healthy reset, not a thesis-breaker.
