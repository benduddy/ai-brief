Solar took the heaviest damage, and three names fell out of the leader grades without breaking their long-term trends.

What happened

The index fell for a second day on bond yields: S&P 500 7,706 (−0.76%), Dow −1.03%, Nasdaq −0.69%. The 10-year yield touched ~5.15% intraday; the 30-year hit its highest since 2004. As on Wednesday, the damage inside our universe was not even — but the winners changed. Wednesday bought memory and sold the spenders; Thursday bought the neoclouds (NBIS +7.4%, CRWV +3.7%) and crypto tokens (+4.3% on average), and sold solar (−4.1%) and part of the memory run. Six names changed status, down from thirteen.

Chips were split, not sold

The semiconductor group averaged only −0.6%, and the spread inside it was wide: AMD +2.4% to a fresh high and Intel +3.9%, against Arm −7.9% on its own news. The core AI silicon barely moved (NVDA −0.4%, TSM +1.0%). Read plainly: the pressure is on long-duration and rate-sensitive names — solar project finance, high-multiple stories — not on the AI demand story itself.

Theme vs index

Worth noting the gap: the S&P is about 1.2% below its record high, while the 65-name AI universe sits about 19.7% below its own 60-day high, on average. (Caveat: the live average covers today's universe, not the one the historical drawdown figures used — the two are not one series.)

Re-rating row (R)

Two names pass both legs: near a 52-week high and forward earnings estimates rising. NVDA — estimates up ~20.7% over 60 days while its forward P/E fell ~10.9%, plus a 6.2% earnings beat on 26 Aug with the price held above the pre-report close. That is the strongest form: the stock gets cheaper as it rises. ANET — estimates up ~14.4%, forward P/E flat (+1.8%). AMD and ETN also sit near new highs, but with no estimate trigger — that is momentum, not a re-rating. ⚠️ These thresholds are judgement, not backtested; forward-EPS history only starts 15 Jun 2026.

What the smart money is saying (from the wiki)

Nothing new since Wednesday's brief. InvestAnswers (23 Sep) still reads the Q4 rally as pulled forward into September, and concedes AMD and Micron broke above his range calls — AMD has now added another 2.4% above that. Jordi Visser (20 Sep) has cut AI to ~20% of his book in favour of crypto and silver, while arguing the AI trade is not rate-sensitive. Thursday is a partial test of that claim: the index fell on rates, but the AI names with fresh news (NBIS, CRWV, AMD) rose through it. The two sources still diverge on positioning, not on the long-term story.
