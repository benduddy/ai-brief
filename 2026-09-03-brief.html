Valuation

At ~2.96, Dell is expensive relative to its own modest forward EPS growth: the move here is a revenue-and-mix story, not a cheap-growth story.

Forward view

The AI-server TAM is still expanding and Dell is clearly winning share of it, so revenue can keep climbing. But the market is already paying up on the print; with margins compressing, the forward question is whether AI-server profitability improves — not whether the revenue is there. Today's price already discounts a lot of the good news.
