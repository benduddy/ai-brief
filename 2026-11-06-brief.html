The regime

Credit is worsening: high-yield spreads are 3.32%, up 0.33 points in 50 days. The dollar is 2.3% above its 200-day average. The VIX is 26.8. Real 10-year yield is 2.64%. Gold is not diverging from rates. The credit and rate legs both use real FRED series, so the call rests on real data and not on a proxy. The framework says this is the scenario that most directly contradicts a rotation into BTC. Calibration: this gate catches big drawdowns early but fires often, with close to no edge over the base rate. Read it as conditions that rhyme with past stress episodes, not as a sell signal. A neutral regime is not an all-clear either.

The tape

S&P 500 7,354, 6.0% under its high. 67 of 68 tracked names fell against the 5 Nov snapshot, by 9.9% on average. NVDA closed on its low (the close sits at 1% of the day's range) and swung 2.3 normal days from high to low. I searched for the cause and found no report for this session. Earlier 2026 chip sell-offs (5 Jun, 17 Jul, 28 Jul) were tied to rate fears, AI-spending worries and Nvidia-financing fears, but I cannot say that any of those applies today. This is the one thing to find out before reading too much into the numbers.

Flexible-load watch

No named new adopter, no ERCOT registration for an AI load and no non-ERCOT tariff found. What the smart money is saying (wiki, newest page 18 Oct, 19 days old and written before this drop)
