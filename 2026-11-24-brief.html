Flexible-load watch

Hit, diffusion direction (everyone can connect sooner, which is mildly bearish for the equipment names GEV, ETN, BE, VRT). Per Modo Energy, around 16 Nov PJM filed three new service classes (and one 12-month combined study, half its current cycle), MISO three (its curtailable class interrupts above $100/MWh), NYISO four (non-firm load bids capped at $200/MWh), ISO-NE three; MISO, NYISO and ISO-NE propose 90-day studies. CAISO filed a large-load tariff amendment (ER27-506) that reportedly leaves the flexible class to its utilities. SPP not confirmed. Caveats: secondary source, dockets not read, FERC has not ruled (PJM answers due 16 Dec). No named site, energisation date or hyperscaler curtailment hours yet — so no adopter hit.
