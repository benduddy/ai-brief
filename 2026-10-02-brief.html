What happened

The S&P 500 rose 0.19% to 7,666.45. The Nasdaq rose 0.04% and the Dow 0.04%. Indices dipped to session lows when long-dated yields rose, then recovered. Macro regime: neutral, unchanged. The credit read (high-yield spread 3.12, up ~0.38 over 50 days) is worsening on the model's own flag, but the regime gate does not fire on it alone. Treat that as context, not a call.

Theme vs index

Worth noting the gap: the S&P is about 1.7% below its high, while the 65-name AI universe sits about 19.0% below its own 60-day high, on average. (Caveat: the live average covers today's universe, not the one the historical drawdown figures used. The two are not one series.)

Yield-curve gate (G1)

The 2s10s curve (10-year yield minus 2-year yield) rose to 0.46 from 0.41, crossing the ~0.45 line. Its 50-day change is +0.06 (steepening), with no sign flip. The gate opens only on a bull-steepening, where short yields fall faster than long ones. I have not verified which end moved, so status stays shut until the next print. Gate open would mean liquidity can broaden into BTC and small caps. Gate shut does not mean the AI thesis is broken.

BTC value read

BTC is $85,882, above its 200-day (~$71,328). MVRV (market price ÷ average holder cost) is 1.58 now, after a low of 1.10 on 30 Jun. That is the 'cheap, then confirmed' pattern that worked in 3 of 4 past bear markets (it failed in 2014). It describes the recovery already made, not where price goes next.

Optics supply chain

The group's day was a news day, not a data day: a Bernstein initiation on LITE ($1,220 target) and COHR ($350), a Citi target lift on LITE to $1,400, and the China-transceiver headline. A ban would help Western makers on volume. It would also raise the cost of the indium phosphide inputs the Western makers still source from China (a point from the August coverage). See LITE and COHR below.

Deal checks and flexible-load watch

AAOI: no new design win or customer change; the stock's +8% day came with a press-reported new 800G hyperscale order, which I could not tie to a named customer, so it is not a hit. CLSK: the Meta-linked Sandersville lease is already baseline; no GPU-rental deal, Texas still an LOI. Financing is the new angle: the project's 7.875% notes and a tightening bond market for low-rated borrowers. Flexible-load watch: no new adopter and no non-ERCOT tariff.

Leadership, falsifiers

Leadership line silent (written yesterday, no sign change). IGV is 108.2, near the ~110 level David Hunter named as a possible top. That is his level, not one the model derived. F1 GPU-rental index is still too short to read (insufficient). Monthly F1/F2/F3 checks were done on 1 Oct and all read intact.
