Macro regime

In the rotation framework this is the “deflationary bust” column, where a rotation into BTC fails — and BTC is down again, to ~$61.8k. The gate fires often with close to no edge, so read it as “conditions rhyme with past stress”. Lagging context: NFCI −0.48 as of 13 Nov (negative = looser than average).

It opened ~$110–111.50 after a delayed start and closed $97.17, +17%, about $1.27T market value (Forbes, NBC, Fast Company; opening print differs by source). But the chip names that sell into that demand fell harder than the market: China risk (AMD short report, Nvidia’s China curbs) and governance (Nvidia CEO change) now drive the price, not demand. F3 (hyperscaler spend) is not touched by any of this.

ATR = the name’s normal daily swing. Forward PEG = price/earnings divided by expected growth. Entry grades A/B/C pass both momentum gates; the letter shows where price sits against short-term averages and is not a rank. D = failed a gate.
