Market backdrop

the tape, the smart money, and the index gap
The setting

Tape

US indices near record highs — the S&P 500 sits around 7,750, within a few percent of its all-time high — with a cooler July CPI (released 12 Aug) helping sentiment. Semiconductors are firm (SMH up ~6.5% mid-month) and, notably, leadership is broadening beyond Nvidia into networking, optical and the neoclouds. Today's move was risk-on and wide, not a single-name spike.

What the smart money is saying (from the wiki)

Jordi Visser (9 Aug, "the AI crash is over") calls the bottom on probability and keeps a preference for infrastructure over hyperscalers — Goldman shows hyperscaler free cash flow negative for years, while the supply layers (memory, optical, power) keep pricing in real demand. Milk Road / Kyle + Vincent (11 Aug) read Nvidia's ~$500B consortium as easing the financing mismatch that broke the neoclouds earlier this summer — "very bullish" near-term — but flag the structural catch: neoclouds capture only ~0.2¢ of every $1 of model spend, the thinnest take in the stack, so the long-term survivor list stays short (CoreWeave, Crusoe, SpaceX, the hyperscalers themselves). InvestAnswers (5 Aug): V-shaped bounce confirmed, S&P P/E ~20, "no bubble." Where they diverge: Milk Road is bullish on the financing fix; Jordi is sceptical of the neoclouds' own economics even as he likes the layers above them.

The index gap (context, not a signal)

Worth noting the gap: the S&P is near record highs while the 58-name AI universe sits about 33% below its own 60-day highs on average. That average now sweeps in the newer, deliberately lower-beta Application-Layer names (TRV/ALL/CB/KRE), so it is not directly comparable to the −44%/−50% equal-weight figures from the 9 Aug backtest — quote today's number for today's gap, not as one series with the history.
