What happened

The S&P 500 fell 0.25% to 7,651.54, the Nasdaq rose 0.24% to 26,861.06 and the Dow fell 0.86% to 50,906.05. Yields stayed at multi-year highs (10-year ~5.29%, 30-year ~5.63%). The VIX read ~16.7. Macro regime: neutral, unchanged. Micron's results arrived after the close, so Thursday's open is the first real test of them.

Theme vs index

Worth noting the gap: the S&P is about 1.9% below its high, while the 65-name AI universe sits about 20.1% below its own 60-day high, on average. (Caveat: the live average covers today's universe, not the one the historical drawdown figures used. The two are not one series.)

Yield-curve gate (G1)

The 2s10s curve (the 10-year yield minus the 2-year yield) is 0.41, up from 0.37. Its 50-day change flipped sign from −0.03 to +0.01, so the trend turned from flattening to marginally steepening. That is why this line appears. The gate opens only on a bull-steepening through ~0.45 points (the short end falling faster than the long end). Gate open would mean liquidity can broaden into BTC and small caps. Gate shut is the expected state and does not say the AI thesis is broken.

Leadership scorecard

Semiconductors have outrun financials by ~8.8 points and materials by ~6.6 points over the last 50 sessions, with semis themselves up ~4.3%. High-yield bond spreads are 3.08 points, up ~0.35 over 50 days. This is a scorecard on David Hunter's late-cycle script, not a signal of our own: one leg only, so no conclusion.

Monthly falsifier check

F1 (GPU rental): intact. Our weekly on-demand index is only one week old (still too short to read). Press checks show prices holding, with the usual 3x gap between hyperscaler and neocloud rates. F2 (DRAM spot): intact. Spot premiums persist and Micron's guide points up, not down. F3 (capex language): intact. No hyperscaler cut in words; next test is the late-October earnings round. A fire would be a ‘pay attention’ flag, not an exit signal.

Deal checks and flexible-load watch

AAOI: no new design win or customer change this month. The >$200M 1.6T hyperscaler order dates from 9 March; deliveries start very late in Q3 and ramp in Q4. CLSK: the Sandersville tenant (Meta, via Anviran; 20-year, $6.6B, 175 MW) is already in our baseline, the Texas site (885 MW) is still a letter of intent, and there is no GPU-rental deal. Flexible-load watch: no new adopter, no non-ERCOT tariff filed. ERCOT Batch Zero is still stalled by the state audit in our notes; one press item mentions provisional classifications on 3 Sep, which I could not confirm.

Re-rating row (R)
