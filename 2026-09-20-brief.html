What happened

The Fed raised rates 25bps on 16 September and signalled that further hikes are likely. August CPI, released 11 September, held at 3.4% year-on-year but core rose +0.3% on the month — the fastest since April. Semis wobbled early in the week on AI-safety headlines, then recovered. Against that, Friday's move in this universe was risk-on in a narrow way: storage and miners, not the broad complex.

Why memory moved

No single company event explains it. Sandisk's move lines up with index-inclusion flows around the 21 September rebalance, and the group has been running on AI-driven storage demand all year. Treat Friday as momentum and flow rather than news — and note that Bloom Energy, which also carries an index add, fell 5.4%, so the flow story is not a clean rule.

Why miners moved

Bitcoin network difficulty eased, and miner equities outran bitcoin itself (BTC roughly +1.4%). JPMorgan re-rated the group toward AI/HPC infrastructure — upgrading CIFR and CLSK while trimming MARA and RIOT targets. The move is a re-rating of what these companies are becoming, not of what they currently earn: both MARA and CLSK posted double-digit revenue declines year-on-year at their last prints.

Macro regime

Neutral, and unchanged. High-yield spreads sit at 2.70 and are not widening, the 10-year real rate is 2.61%, VIX 14.8. The 2s10s curve is +0.25, flatter by ~0.16 over 50 sessions — consistent with a hiking Fed. Credit and rates both read from the real FRED series today, so this call does not rest on a proxy. Neutral is the expected output most days; it is not an all-clear.

The index gap

Worth stating plainly and drawing no conclusion from: the S&P 500 is about 1.9% below its record high, while the 58-name AI universe sits about 26.1% below its own 60-day high, on average. Index-level reassurance does not transfer to this book.

What the smart money is saying (from the wiki)

IA (9 Sep) took profits into CleanSpark's run from ~$10.80 toward ~$14 — the level the stock closed at on Friday — while still arguing the structural point that miners holding bitcoin on the balance sheet no longer have to sell it to pay bills. Jordi Visser (10 Sep) says the AI trade has resumed but expects nothing like the earlier slope, with Micron the memory anchor. The tension worth holding: the signal model has CLSK Leading and buyable-on-strength, while the source Ben follows was selling into exactly this price.
