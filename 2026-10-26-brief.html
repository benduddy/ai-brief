Momentum = strength of the recent up-move. Status: Leading = strong and healthy; Overheated = strong and stretched (not a pullback signal); Basing = weak, not yet a bottom; Neutral = no clear signal. ATR = the name’s normal daily swing. Forward PEG = forward P/E ÷ expected EPS growth; under ~1 cheap for its growth, over ~2 expensive. Entry grades A/B/C: the name clears both momentum gates; the letter is position against short-term averages, not a rank. D: no momentum edge yet.

Sources: local signal files (Yahoo, FRED, DefiLlama/CoinGecko on-chain), Investing wiki, and web searches (Lam Research release of 21 Oct and an extract of it; Bloom Energy release summary; Reuters via The Star for the 19 Oct chip recap; 247WallSt and TIKR for neocloud and miner context; Latitude Media and law-firm summaries for the flexible-load docket).
