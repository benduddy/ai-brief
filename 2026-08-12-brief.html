Wednesday Signal Brief

12 August 2026 · 50 names · benchmark QQQ · data Tue 11 Aug close · 07:28 UTC

The optical leaders that led last week's bounce cooled hard into their own earnings — and the power, grid and lithography names took the baton. Inside the AI-supplier basket, the pieces are starting to move on their own clocks.

  • The rotation is now inside the supplier basket. The optical/transceiver names that led Friday's rebound gave a big chunk back — Coherent (COHR) −13% over two sessions, Lumentum (LITE) −8.6%, both dropping from Leading to Basing — while electrical/grid power (Eaton ETN, Siemens Energy ENR.DE) and chip-making gear (ASML) firmed to fresh highs. This is the "your capex is my opportunity" trade splintering: the parts of the AI build-out no longer move as one.
  • The give-back looks like positioning, not a crack. Lumentum actually beat when it reported Tuesday (revenue +109% year-on-year, adjusted EPS $3.23 vs $2.99 expected, strong next-year guide) and still fell — a classic sell-the-news after a huge run. Coherent reports tonight (after the close), so its drop is de-risking ahead of a binary print, not a signal about the business.
  • The pullback bullseye is unusually full. Thirteen names are still leading and reasonably cheap for their growth (low forward PEG) — and that list now includes both beaten-down optical names, LITE (entry grade A, PEG ~0.45) and COHR (grade B, PEG ~0.73). Nothing in the leading set screens as expensive today.
  • Value-accumulation watch: Micron (MU) sits back near $869 — inside Jordi Visser's old sub-$900 buy zone — with HBM memory sold out through 2026 (forward PEG ~0.05, next-year EPS growth ~+111%). It's the standout "quiet tape, strong future" name, joined by AMD and Astera Labs (ALAB) in compute and GE Vernova (GEV), Vertiv (VRT) and Bloom Energy (BE) in data-center power.
  • The index-vs-portfolio gap: the S&P 500 is testing a record around 7,728, while the 50-name AI universe sits ~40% below its own 60-day highs on average. Marquee event ahead: Nvidia Q2 on 26 Aug; CPI lands today, PPI tomorrow.
