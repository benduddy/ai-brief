One partial tell: the Nextracker quarter included behind-the-meter storage for a hyperscaler's AI data centre. That points to storage bought for speed, but the customer and the interconnection reason are not stated. Counter-evidence: one analysis argues price-based curtailment payments will not motivate hyperscalers.

Deal checks

What the smart money is saying (wiki, newest page ~18 Oct, so about 18 days old)

Jordi Visser: the October sell-off is hedge-fund deleveraging, not a demand bust; expects pain into hyperscaler earnings, then a snapback by year-end. InvestAnswers (14 Oct): found nothing to buy, blames AI-credit fear, and expects "something blows up"; his top tier is Broadcom, ALAB and MRVL. The two diverge: positioning versus credit. The wiki's own scenario odds (18 Oct): shakeout then recovery ~35%, grind ~40%, reset or credit event ~25%. The wiki's test was whether NVDA rises after the late-October capex raises. It has not, which leans toward grind or reset (my inference). Both sources predate the Marvell–Lumentum deal.

How to read this. Neutral, balanced read — bull and bear side by side. The Setup lines are opinions on risk/reward and timing to weigh for yourself, not directives; the call is yours.

Score key. Heat: 0 = far below its highs and its 50-day (damaged; historically these fell further), 100 = a strong run near its highs (historically no extra pullback risk). Momentum: strength of the recent up-move. Status: Leading = strong and healthy, Overheated = strong and stretched (not a pullback signal), Basing = weak, not yet a bottom, Neutral = no clear signal. ATR: the name's normal daily swing. Forward PEG: forward P/E divided by expected EPS growth; under ~1 is cheap for the growth, over ~2 expensive. Entry grades A/B/C all pass the trend and relative-strength gates; the letter shows where the price sits against its short-term averages and is not a rank. Grade thresholds for the re-rating row are judgement, not backtested.
