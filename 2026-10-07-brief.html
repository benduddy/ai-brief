Bullish

At its Investor Day on Tuesday, Marvell raised its fiscal 2028 revenue target to about $20B, from the earlier $18B and above the ~$18.2B analysts had pencilled in. It also lifted its 2028 data-center market estimate to ~$94B (35% a year), with custom chips and the parts that attach to them as the fastest-growing slice. Bearish

The new target needs custom-chip programs to land on schedule, and those depend on a few hyperscaler customers. The stock is up ~25% above its 50-day, so a lot of the good news is already in the price. Marvell flipped Leading → Overheated on the move.

Valuation

Forward P/E 42.5, next-year EPS growth +60%, forward PEG 0.71 (forward P/E ÷ expected growth; under ~1 means you pay little for a lot of growth). That reads cheap for its growth.

Forward view

The $20B target is the forward view: it is the company's own claim, not a consensus yet. Analysts will reset estimates this week; today's price already discounts part of it, not all.
