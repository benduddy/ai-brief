Market backdrop & what the smart money is saying

Backdrop

Tape

Tuesday was a rotation into the AI complex: the semiconductor ETF (SOXX) rose ~1.7% and the biggest gains were in energy, power and technology, while healthcare and financials led the declines. The proximate spark was OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra (released 3 Sep) — read as another compute scale-up cycle that points straight at 800G/1.6T optical modules, which is why the optical names (LITE, GLW, COHR) led. Volatility stayed low (VIX ~16).

This week's macro

CPI and PPI land later this week. With the Warsh Fed still some way from its 2% target, an upside inflation surprise is the clear risk — it would make it hard for the Fed not to lean hawkish into next week's meeting. That is the event that could interrupt a hot semis tape.

Macro regime

Still debasement, ~day six and unchanged — so background, not a headline. The signature is the soft version: gold sits ~1.8% below its 200-day average even as the real 10-year yield has risen, so the read rests on the gold-vs-real-yield divergence (on the real FRED series) plus a soft dollar. Credit is calm (high-yield spread ~2.68%, tightening slightly) and the 2s10s curve is +0.41, so the liquidity "gate" stays shut (below the ~0.45 trigger). Treat it as "conditions still rhyme with debasement," not a signal to act.

Smart money — from the wiki

The followed commentators still favour infrastructure and the compute layer over the hyperscalers themselves — and Tuesday's tape (neoclouds + semis + optical + power leading, mega-caps like NVDA lagging) is that call in action. Jordi Visser grounds it in a "compute-centralisation" argument (Dylan Patel / SemiAnalysis, relayed 30 Aug): the labs with the highest revenue-per-megawatt corner scarce compute, and CoreWeave is "the public-market expression" of that scarce physical layer. Firm GPU-rental prices say the scarcity is intact (falsifier F1 still intact).

Standing checks

Liquidity gate G1 stays shut (2s10s +0.41, flat) — silent. The monthly falsifier check (F1 GPU rental, F2 DRAM spot) and the AAOI/CLSK deal checks are next due ~1 Oct; a spot look this run confirms no change — CleanSpark's Sandersville tenant is still unnamed and Texas is still an LOI, and AAOI has no new named design win or lost customer (record Q2 revenue $191.9M, YTD +203%, a $300M Pearland expansion, plus a $600M raise).
